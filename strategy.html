What this is

ORB v3 is an opening-range breakout day-trading system on liquid Nasdaq names. After the first hour of the regular session, it waits for the first close beyond that hour’s high (long) or low (short), then places a stop and a take-profit and exits the same day.

How trades are entered / executed

Signal

  • Long: first bar close above ORH after the OR
  • Short: first bar close below ORL after the OR
  • Mode: first break only (one attempt per symbol per day)

Scanner cadence

Weekday Grok Bot routines poll about every 15 minutes (cron minutes 7, 22, 37, 52) in the 6:00 AM – ~12:00 PM PT window, then manage toward flatten near the close. LIVE and PAPER use separate routines. LIVE chat heartbeats are mandatory; PAPER chat notes prefer arms/fills only (lighter). Slack #grok-trades is actionable-only for both — label LIVE or PAPER.

Orders, state, sizing

  • Places an Alpaca bracket (or equivalent): entry + stop + take-profit
  • LIVE state: orb_v3_live_state.json · PAPER state: orb_v3_paper_state.json (never mixed)
  • LIVE secrets: YOPC live-secrets.json · PAPER secrets: YOPC paper-secrets.json (never paste keys; paper never falls back to live)
  • LIVE + PAPER cash-only: max 16% equity notional per symbol; max 6 concurrent (fits in cash). Cash gate holds entries cash cannot cover (skip_insufficient_cash). No margin.
  • PAPER uses the same 6 / 16% sizing as LIVE; NDX-100 universe and paper secrets stay separate. Older larger PAPER lots stay until they exit.
  • Risk 1.5% of equity per trade → shares ≈ risk $ / R

How stop and target are set

PieceRule
StopAlways the opening-range midpoint
R|entry − stop|
TargetEntry ± 2.5R (long up / short down)
Trail / scale-outNone — all-in / all-out

Long example

ORH = 120, ORL = 116 → mid (stop) = 118 Breakout long at 120.20 → R = 120.20 − 118 = 2.20 Target = 120.20 + 2.5 × 2.20 = 125.70 Stop = 118.00 → stop ≈ −1R · target ≈ +2.5R (before costs)

Short example

Same OR: ORH = 120, ORL = 116 → mid (stop) = 118 Breakout short at 115.80 → R = 118 − 115.80 = 2.20 Target = 115.80 − 2.5 × 2.20 = 110.30 Stop = 118.00 (above entry)

How trades are exited

PriorityExitEffect
1Stop hitClose at OR midpoint → loss ≈ −1R
2Target hitClose at +2.5R
3EOD flattenStill open near close → flatten ~12:50 PT
4Daily haltDay P/L ≤ −2R → stop new trades that day

No overnight. No second trade on a symbol already traded that day.
